About

Ben is a waterman with a civil engineering background & years of experience in pre-contract and project delivery for marine civil and dredging projects.

He has worked across the full project lifecycle, from preparing tenders and planning the works through to delivery on site and handover. With an understanding of the pressures marine contractors face, he has set out to offer support to keep projects organised, compliant and moving forward.

Having spent his life in and around the water, he is passionate about improving coastal environments by offering marine-specific engineering and management support.
